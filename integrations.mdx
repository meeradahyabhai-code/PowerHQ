Three ways to put energy shopping in front of your customers. They differ in one dimension: **how
much you build versus how much control you keep.** Everything behind the checkout is identical.

| | Affiliate link | Embedded iFrame | Plan Data API |
|---|---|---|---|
| **You build** | Nothing | Nothing | Your own UI |
| **Engineering needed** | None | None | Yes |
| **Where the customer shops** | On PowerHQ | On your site, in an embed | In your product |
| **Branding** | PowerHQ | Your fonts and colors | Entirely yours |
| **Control of the experience** | None | Layout and brand | Complete |
| **Time to live** | Days | Days | Weeks |
| **Checkout and enrollment** | PowerHQ | PowerHQ | PowerHQ-hosted |
| **Compliance and PII** | PowerHQ | PowerHQ | PowerHQ |
| **Upfront cost** | $0 | $0 | $0 |

<Info>
  You are not locked in. Partners commonly start with a link or an embed to prove the channel
  converts, then move to the API once energy earns a permanent place in the product.
</Info>

## Affiliate link

**Share one link. Nothing to build.**

You get a personal referral link carrying your partner code. Post it on your site, email it, or share
it in person. When someone you refer shops and enrolls, the sign-up is credited to you.

There is no website requirement, no plugin and no developer. If you can paste a link, you're ready.
Most affiliates are set up and sharing within the same week.

**Best when** you have an audience but not a product surface to embed into — a newsletter, a
community, a brokerage, a services business.

## Embedded iFrame

**Our shopping experience, inside your site, in your brand.**

The iFrame embeds PowerHQ's energy shopping and enrollment flow directly into your page. Visitors
compare plans, sign up and complete enrollment without leaving your site. We configure fonts and
colors to match, and customer emails can carry your name.

The experience adapts to the customer's market automatically, supports residential and business by
default, and is continually optimized for conversion — you inherit that work rather than doing it.
There is nothing for your engineering team to build.

**Best when** you want energy live on your own site quickly, and the shopping UX is not where you
want to spend engineering time.

<Note>
  The iFrame test environment is access-restricted, so onboarding collects the IP addresses your team
  will test from. This applies to the test site only — it is not a requirement of the API.
</Note>

## Plan Data API

**You render the plans. We run everything behind checkout.**

A single GraphQL endpoint returns residential and business plans for any deregulated ZIP code, with
component-level pricing, supplier scores, disclosure documents and a ready-to-use enrollment URL per
plan. You build the interface exactly as you want it; when the customer picks a plan, you hand them to
the PowerHQ-hosted checkout and we take it from there — contract, eSign, credit, fulfillment.

One endpoint, always `POST`, your key in the `x-api-key` header. Ask for exactly the fields you need
and get back just those.

**Best when** the energy experience needs to live natively inside your product, or you want to rank,
filter and present plans against data you already hold about the customer.

## What every path has in common

- **PowerHQ owns the regulated parts.** Supplier contracts, licensing, state-by-state compliance,
  enrollment operations and customer service.
- **Checkout is hosted by PowerHQ**, so payment and personal data never land in your systems.
- **Every deregulated market**, residential and business, from one integration.
- **$0 upfront.** Compensation terms are set in Exhibit A of your partner agreement.
